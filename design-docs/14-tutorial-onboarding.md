# 14 — Tutorial & Onboarding

## Overview
Ultimate frisbee has complex rules and strategies that are unfamiliar to most gamers. FrisKingdom's tutorial system introduces mechanics progressively — from basic movement to advanced strategy — through a structured series of interactive lessons. A practice mode provides free-form training, and contextual hints offer guidance during real games.

Related docs: [03-ultimate-rules-gameplay](03-ultimate-rules-gameplay.md), [04-player-controls](04-player-controls.md), [09-ai-system](09-ai-system.md)

## Tutorial Philosophy
1. **Learn by doing**: Every tutorial lesson is interactive, not text-based
2. **Progressive complexity**: Start simple (movement), build to complex (strategy)
3. **No walls of text**: Instructions are 1–2 sentences max, shown during gameplay
4. **Repeatable**: Any lesson can be replayed from the tutorial menu
5. **Skippable**: Experienced players can skip the entire tutorial or individual lessons
6. **Contextual**: After tutorial, hints appear in real games when relevant situations arise (optional)

## Tutorial Structure

### Chapter 1: Movement Basics
**Goal**: Player can move, sprint, and switch players.

| Lesson | Duration | Content |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 1.1 Move Around | 30s | Left stick / WASD to move. Run to each cone marker. |
| 1.2 Sprint | 30s | Hold sprint button. Sprint through a set of gates within a time limit. |
| 1.3 Player Switching | 45s | Switch between 3 teammates using RB. Switch to the highlighted player. |
| 1.4 Stamina | 30s | Sprint until stamina depletes. Notice the slowdown. Wait for recovery. |

**Completion**: All lessons complete → Chapter 2 unlocked.
**Practice drill**: Free movement around an empty field with all teammates.

### Chapter 2: Throwing
**Goal**: Player can throw backhand and forehand with basic aim and power control.

| Lesson | Duration | Content |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 2.1 Backhand Basics | 60s | Press A/Cross for a quick backhand. Throw to a stationary target. Hit 3/5 targets. |
| 2.2 Forehand Basics | 60s | Press X/Square for a quick forehand. Throw to targets on the other side. Hit 3/5. |
| 2.3 Power Control | 90s | Hold RT to charge, release to throw. Hit near (low power), middle, and far (high power) targets. |
| 2.4 Aim Control | 90s | Use right stick to aim. Hit targets at different horizontal angles. |
| 2.5 The Sweet Spot | 60s | Practice hitting the sweet spot (70–85% power). Visual feedback shows sweet spot zone. |
| 2.6 Pivoting | 60s | With disc, use left stick to pivot. Throw to targets at different angles around the pivot. |

**Completion**: Successfully complete all throwing challenges.
**Practice drill**: Throwing range with target rings at various distances and angles. Score displayed.

### Chapter 3: Catching
**Goal**: Player can catch discs thrown to them, including layout catches.

| Lesson | Duration | Content |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 3.1 Auto-Catch | 45s | Stand near the catch zone. Teammate throws to you. Catch is automatic. Catch 5 throws. |
| 3.2 Running Catch | 60s | Move to the highlighted zone before the disc arrives. Catch while running. 5 catches. |
| 3.3 Layout Catch | 60s | Disc thrown just out of reach. Press RT/Space to dive. Make 3 layout catches. |
| 3.4 Sky Catch | 45s | High disc thrown. Press Y/Triangle to jump. Time the jump to catch. 3 sky catches. |

**Completion**: All catch types demonstrated.
**Practice drill**: "Catch machine" — teammate throws various passes, player catches them. Difficulty ramps.

### Chapter 4: Disc Flight & Advanced Throws
**Goal**: Player understands how disc flight works and can use specialty throws.

| Lesson | Duration | Content |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 4.1 Hyzer & Anhyzer | 90s | Adjust release angle with left stick. See how hyzer curves left (RHBH) and anhyzer curves right. Hit curved targets. |
| 4.2 Wind Effects | 90s | Wind is blowing. Same throw, notice the drift. Adjust aim to compensate. Hit targets in wind. |
| 4.3 The Hammer | 60s | Press Y/Triangle for overhead throw. Notice the high arc and fade. Hit targets behind obstacles. |
| 4.4 Inside-Out & Outside-In | 90s | Combine aim + release angle for IO/OI throws. Hit targets behind the mark. |
| 4.5 Break Throws | 60s | A mark (defender) is set. Throw to the "break" side using different throw types. |

**Completion**: Demonstrate each throw type successfully.
**Practice drill**: Obstacle course with targets that require specific throw types (hyzer around a wall, hammer over a defender, etc.).

### Chapter 5: Rules of Ultimate
**Goal**: Player understands the core rules (scoring, turnovers, stall count, pulls).

| Lesson | Duration | Content |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 5.1 Scoring | 60s | Catch the disc in the end zone to score. Walk through a simple scoring scenario. |
| 5.2 The Pull | 60s | Start of play: throwing team pulls. Practice throwing a pull. Then receive a pull. |
| 5.3 Turnovers | 90s | Demonstrate: drop → turnover. Out of bounds → turnover. Stall out → turnover. Each resets possession. |
| 5.4 The Stall Count | 60s | Hold the disc. Watch the stall count rise. Must throw before 10. Practice under stall pressure. |
| 5.5 The Mark & Force | 60s | A defender marks you. Notice the "force" — they block one side. Throw to the open side. |
| 5.6 Fouls | 45s | Contact during a throw = foul. Disc goes back. Brief demonstration. |

**Completion**: Play a simplified 3v3 mini-game (first to 3 points) applying all rules learned.
**Practice drill**: Rule scenario mode — random situations presented, player must resolve correctly.

### Chapter 6: Offense
**Goal**: Player understands offensive formations and cutting.

| Lesson | Duration | Content |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 6.1 Vert Stack Basics | 90s | Shown a vert stack setup. As a cutter: cut in when prompted, then clear. Score from a stack play. |
| 6.2 Handler Movement | 90s | As a handler: receive the disc, look for open cutters, throw to them. Reset when no one's open. |
| 6.3 Give & Go | 60s | Throw the disc, then immediately cut. Receive the return pass. Score. |
| 6.4 Horiz Stack | 60s | Shown a horiz stack. Cutter isolation — beat your defender 1v1. |
| 6.5 Calling Plays | 45s | Use D-pad to call formations. Teammates respond. Practice calling vert and horiz. |

**Completion**: Win a 7v7 game to 5 points using learned offensive concepts.

### Chapter 7: Defense
**Goal**: Player understands defensive mechanics and strategies.

| Lesson | Duration | Content |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 7.1 Marking (The Force) | 60s | Stand on the correct side to set the force. React to fakes. Maintain position. |
| 7.2 Downfield Defense | 60s | Guard a cutter. Stay between them and the disc. React to their cuts. |
| 7.3 Getting a D (Block) | 60s | Position near a throw lane. Press X/Square for hand block. Get 2 blocks. |
| 7.4 The Layout D | 45s | Dive for a block on a throw just out of reach. Press B/Circle to bid. |
| 7.5 Zone Defense Intro | 90s | Position in a cup. Move with the disc. Shift as a unit. Practice zone shape. |

**Completion**: Force 3 turnovers in a 7v7 defensive possession drill.

### Chapter 8: Advanced Strategy (Optional)
**Goal**: Deeper strategic understanding for competitive play.

| Lesson | Duration | Content |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 8.1 Reading the Field | 90s | Freeze-frame analysis: identify open cutters, optimal throws, defensive weaknesses. |
| 8.2 Huck Game | 60s | Practice deep throws. Read wind. Time hucks with cutter movement. |
| 8.3 Zone Offense | 90s | Against a zone defense: swing the disc, find soft spots, attack gaps. |
| 8.4 Clam Defense | 60s | Switch between man and zone on the fly. Communication indicators. |
| 8.5 End Game Strategy | 60s | Game point scenarios: conservative offense, aggressive defense, timeout management. |

## Practice Mode

Practice mode is a free-form training environment accessible from the main menu:

### Practice Menu
```
Practice Mode
├── Free Play (empty field, any throws, no defenders)
├── Throwing Range (targets at distances, score tracking)
├── Catch Machine (receives passes of varying difficulty)
├── 1v1 Drills (one-on-one offense vs defense)
├── 3v3 Mini Game (small field, quick games)
├── Pull Practice (practice kickoff throws)
├── Replay Tutorial (select any chapter to replay)
└── Sandbox (full 7v7, adjustable AI, no score pressure)
```

### Throwing Range Details
```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│   [10m]    [20m]    [30m]    [40m]    [50m]    │
│     ○        ○        ○        ○        ○      │
│   [5pts]   [10pts]  [15pts]  [20pts]  [25pts]  │
│                                                 │
│     ○        ○        ○                        │
│  [Break]  [Around] [Through]                   │
│  [10pts]  [15pts]  [20pts]                     │
│                                                 │
│                 [Player]                        │
│                    ↑                            │
│              Wind: → 5 m/s                     │
│                                                 │
│  Score: 145    Best: 210    Streak: 4          │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Targets are ring-based: bullseye (dead center) = full points, outer ring = half points, miss = 0.
Targets can be:
- **Stationary**: Fixed position (for learning)
- **Moving**: Simulating a running cutter (for advanced practice)
- **Timed**: Must hit within a time window (stall pressure)

## Contextual Hints System

After completing (or skipping) the tutorial, contextual hints appear during real games:

### Hint Triggers
| Situation | Hint Text | Display |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| First time holding disc in a game | "Press RT to charge a throw, release to throw" | Bottom of screen, 3s |
| Stall count reaches 7 (first time) | "Stall count is high — throw or call timeout!" | Flashing near stall bar, 2s |
| Player not cutting (standing still on offense) | "Press A to cut in, or X to cut deep" | Subtle, near player, 2s |
| Defense: not marking anyone | "Switch to a nearby defender with RB" | Near player indicator, 2s |
| Turnover (first time) | "Turnover! Your team now has the disc" | Center, 2s |
| Opponent in zone defense (first encounter) | "Zone defense — swing the disc side to side" | Top of screen, 3s |
| Wind is strong (first game with wind) | "Strong wind — adjust your throws accordingly" | Near wind indicator, 3s |

### Hint Settings
- **Hints**: On (all hints) / Minimal (critical only) / Off
- Hints are suppressed after being shown 3 times (player has seen it enough)
- A "?" button on the pause menu shows all available hints for the current situation

## Tutorial State Machine

```rust
#[derive(Resource)]
pub struct TutorialState {
    pub current_chapter: u8,
    pub current_lesson: u8,
    pub lessons_completed: HashSet<(u8, u8)>,
    pub chapters_completed: HashSet<u8>,
    pub hints_shown: HashMap<HintType, u32>,
    pub hints_enabled: HintSetting,
}

#[derive(Resource)]
pub struct ActiveLesson {
    pub chapter: u8,
    pub lesson: u8,
    pub objectives: Vec<Objective>,
    pub current_objective: usize,
    pub instruction_text: String,
    pub time_limit: Option<f32>,
    pub demo_mode: bool,          // true = AI demonstrates first
}

pub struct Objective {
    pub description: String,
    pub check: ObjectiveCheck,     // condition to complete
    pub completed: bool,
}

pub enum ObjectiveCheck {
    ReachPosition(Vec3, f32),       // position, radius
    ThrowToTarget(TargetId),
    CatchDisc,
    LayoutCatch,
    ScoreGoal,
    ForcesTurnover,
    SurviveStallCount(u32),         // hold disc for N stall counts then throw
    CallPlay(PlayCall),
    Custom(fn(&World) -> bool),
}
```

## First-Time Player Flow

When a player starts FrisKingdom for the first time:

```
1. Title screen → "Press Start"
2. Brief cinematic intro (stickman highlights montage, 15 seconds)
3. Prompt: "Would you like to play the tutorial?"
   - "Yes, teach me!" → Tutorial Chapter 1
   - "I know ultimate frisbee" → Skip to Chapter 5 (rules may differ in-game)
   - "Skip tutorial" → Main menu (hints enabled by default)
4. After tutorial completion → Main menu with "Quick Match" highlighted
5. First Quick Match: AI set to Casual difficulty automatically
6. After first game: "Great game! Try Season Mode or Career Mode next?"
```

## Tutorial Audio & Visual Treatment

- Simplified field (fewer objects, clearer markings)
- Slow-motion for key moments (first throw, first catch)
- Highlighted zones (green = target, red = avoid)
- Arrow overlays showing direction of movement/throw
- Sound cues: positive chime for completed objectives, gentle buzz for failures
- Encouraging text: "Nice throw!", "Great catch!", "Almost — try again!"

## Practice Mode AI Settings

In sandbox practice, the player can adjust:
- AI difficulty (Beginner → Spirit)
- AI aggression
- Number of players per team (3v3, 5v5, 7v7)
- Wind conditions
- Whether turnovers reset or play continues
- Enable/disable stall count
- Infinite stamina mode
